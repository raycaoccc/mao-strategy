# Worked Example: Should We Pivot Our B2B SaaS to a New Market Segment?

This example demonstrates the full 5-step consultation workflow applied to a product strategy decision.

---

## Situation

A 30-person B2B SaaS company provides project management tools for construction companies. Revenue has plateaued at $2M ARR for 18 months. The CEO has proposed pivoting to target healthcare project management, where a large potential customer has expressed strong interest. The engineering team is concerned about the complexity of healthcare compliance (HIPAA). The sales team is split — some see opportunity, others worry about abandoning existing customers.

---

## Step 1: Describe the Situation (描述情况)

- **Problem**: Revenue plateau; growth stalled in current market
- **Proposed action**: Pivot to healthcare market segment
- **Stakeholders**:
  - CEO: wants growth, sees healthcare as the opportunity
  - Engineering (8 people): concerned about HIPAA complexity and tech debt from current product
  - Sales (5 people): split — 2 excited about healthcare, 3 worried about losing construction clients
  - Existing customers (150 construction companies): dependent on current product
  - Potential customer (1 large healthcare org): expressed interest but no commitment
- **Constraints**: 18 months of runway remaining; no healthcare domain expertise on team; current product is stable but has significant tech debt
- **Desired outcome**: Resume revenue growth trajectory to $5M ARR within 2 years

---

## Step 2: Identify Contradictions (识别矛盾)

| # | Contradiction | Dominant Aspect |
|---|--------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Growth vs stability** — pursuing new market vs maintaining current revenue | Stability is dominant (current revenue sustains the company) |
| 2 | **Healthcare opportunity vs compliance risk** — large potential market vs HIPAA complexity | Risk is dominant (no expertise, unknown cost) |
| 3 | **Sales team alignment vs division** — need unified strategy vs genuine disagreement | Division is dominant (no consensus) |
| 4 | **New market exploration vs tech debt resolution** — invest forward vs fix foundations | Tech debt is subordinate but growing |
| 5 | **Single large customer vs market validation** — one interested customer vs proven demand | Single customer is dominant (no broader validation) |

**Principal contradiction (主要矛盾)**: **#5 — Single customer vs market validation**. The entire pivot rests on one customer's expressed interest, which is not validated market demand. If this interest is real and representative, contradictions #1-4 can be managed. If it is not, everything else is moot.

**Principal aspect**: The single customer signal is currently driving the decision, but market validation is the lever — without it, the signal is unreliable.

---

## Step 3: Select Frameworks (选择框架)

Based on the principal contradiction (information deficit about market demand):

1. **Investigation Method (调查研究)** — primary framework. The decision rests on unverified assumptions about healthcare demand.
2. **United Front (统一战线)** — secondary. The sales team is divided; alignment is needed before any strategic move.
3. **Protracted Strategy (论持久战)** — tertiary. If healthcare is validated, the transition will take time and needs phased planning.

---

## Step 4: Apply Analysis (应用分析)

### Investigation Method Application

**没有调查就没有发言权** — No investigation, no right to speak about healthcare opportunity.

What must be known before deciding:
- Is the interested healthcare customer representative of a broader market need? → Interview 10+ healthcare organizations
- What is the actual compliance cost? → Consult a HIPAA compliance specialist; get cost estimate
- Are there competitors already serving healthcare PM? → Competitive landscape analysis
- Why has growth stalled in construction? → Is it market saturation or product/sales issues?

**Critical finding**: The team has not investigated why construction revenue plateaued. The pivot impulse may be treating a symptom (revenue stagnation) rather than the disease (which could be sales process, pricing, product gaps, or genuine market saturation).

### United Front Application

**Stakeholder map**:
- Core supporters of pivot: CEO, 2 sales reps (3 people)
- Neutrals: 4 engineers, 1 sales rep (5 people)
- Lean opponents: 4 engineers concerned about HIPAA, 2 sales reps worried about existing customers (6 people)
- Core opponents: None explicitly, but engineering concerns are substantive

**Strategy**: Do not force the pivot decision before investigation. Instead, unite the team around "let's find out" — everyone can agree that investigation precedes commitment. This buys time, reduces conflict, and produces better data.

### Protracted Strategy Application (conditional)

If investigation validates healthcare opportunity:
- **Phase 1 (Strategic defensive, 6 months)**: Build HIPAA knowledge; prototype healthcare-specific features; maintain construction revenue as foundation
- **Phase 2 (Stalemate, 6-12 months)**: Pilot with 3-5 healthcare customers; assess product-market fit; begin dual-market operation
- **Phase 3 (Offensive, 12+ months)**: If validated, shift primary investment to healthcare; maintain construction as cash cow

**战略上藐视，战术上重视**: Strategically, a pivot is achievable — other companies have done it. Tactically, every step must be meticulously planned — HIPAA violations can destroy the company.

---

## Step 5: Propose Action (提出行动)

### Immediate Actions (This Week)
1. **Launch investigation sprint**: 2-week focused investigation of healthcare market + construction plateau causes
2. **Unite the team**: Frame the investigation as "we make data-driven decisions" — removes the divisive pivot/no-pivot binary
3. **Assign investigation tasks**: CEO talks to 10 healthcare prospects; sales lead audits construction sales pipeline; CTO estimates HIPAA compliance cost

### Short-Term Actions (This Month)
4. **Complete investigation**: Compile findings into a decision brief
5. **Hold decision meeting**: Present findings; apply Contradiction Analysis to updated information
6. **If healthcare is validated**: Begin Phase 1 of protracted strategy (HIPAA knowledge building, no customer commitments yet)
7. **If healthcare is not validated**: Redirect energy to diagnosing and fixing construction revenue stagnation

### Long-Term Direction (This Quarter+)
8. **Dual-market if validated**: Operate in both markets during transition; do not abandon construction revenue prematurely
9. **Phase gates**: At 6 months, evaluate — if 3+ healthcare pilots, continue; if <3, reassess

### Review Checkpoints
- **Week 2**: Investigation findings reviewed
- **Month 1**: Decision made; pivot plan or construction growth plan initiated
- **Month 6**: Phase gate review (if pivoting)

### Risks
- Investigation reveals neither healthcare nor construction is viable → consider acquisition or broader strategic reassessment
- The interested healthcare customer loses patience during investigation → acceptable risk; a customer who cannot wait 2 weeks is not a reliable foundation for a pivot
- Principal contradiction shifts: if investigation reveals construction stagnation is due to a fixable sales problem, that becomes the principal contradiction
